How to Use UploaderX

Getting Started: Manage Accounts

Configure at least one Google Account. You can add multiple accounts to manage different channels.

  • Click Add New Account to create a new profile.
  • Give the account a memorable Nickname (e.g., "Gaming Channel").
  • Click Choose File or paste your Google Cloud credentials.
  • Click Authorize to sign in and grant permissions.

Configure Task

Select the publishing account and set your preferred upload speed profile (4MB or 8MB).

Provide Video Details

Select your video files, fill in metadata, set privacy, and start the upload pipeline.
